01 / Identity & Core Purpose

Where Medical Passion Meets Scientific Rigor.

Founded by dedicated medical, pharmacy, and dental students at Ferhat Abbas University Sétif 1, EPIC Club transforms university education through practical bedside skills, research literacy, and grassroots community health.

A student-led movement shaping competent, empathetic future physicians in Sétif.

The clinical journey cannot be confined to lecture halls alone. EPIC Club acts as a catalyst where future healthcare professionals bridge textbook pathology with actual clinical dexterity, simulation training, and public engagement.

Working hand-in-hand with hospital clinicians from CHU Saadna Abdenour and esteemed professors at the Faculty of Medicine, we create safe, state-of-the-art simulation environments, run widespread disease screening caravans across the wilaya of Sétif, and foster research that challenges medical students to question and discover.

"EPIC isn't simply an extracurricular club—it is our operating room for ideas, our simulation lab for leadership, and our family throughout medical school."
EC
Executive Board & Mentors Faculty of Medicine of Sétif · CHU Saadna Abdenour
